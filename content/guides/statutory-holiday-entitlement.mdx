## Start with the working pattern

Most workers have a statutory minimum of 5.6 weeks of paid annual leave. For a regular five-day week this usually means 28 days; bank holidays can count towards that minimum. A contract may provide more.

## Choose the right calculation

Record the leave year, start or leaving date, working pattern and contractual entitlement before calculating. Part-time work needs a proportionate calculation. Irregular-hours and part-year workers have specific rules, so do not apply a standard days-per-week formula to every worker. Use the current GOV.UK calculator and guidance for the relevant pattern.

## Separate entitlement from pay

The amount of time off and the money paid during it are separate questions. Review the holiday-pay rules as well as the entitlement calculation; a correct leave balance does not establish correct holiday pay.

## Make the result understandable

Give the worker a clear calculation in the units used for their pattern, including how bank holidays are handled. Retain the inputs and review the balance when hours change. Escalate carry-over, long-term absence and disputed calculations for advice rather than automatically deleting a balance at year end.
