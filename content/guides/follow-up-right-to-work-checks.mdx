## Set the next action when you check

Some time-limited permissions require a further check. Record the relevant expiry or follow-up date from the prescribed evidence and give the task to a named person. A reminder is an internal control, not proof of continuing permission.

## Review before the deadline

Contact the worker early enough to establish which official checking route applies. Where an outstanding application, appeal or administrative review may preserve permission, follow the Home Office guidance for that situation. Use the Employer Checking Service where required and retain the result. Do not assume that every pending application permits work, or that an expired document alone proves permission has ended.

## Keep decisions separate from reminders

Save the new check evidence, decision date, restrictions and next review action. If the position remains unclear, escalate promptly for qualified advice before making an employment decision. Explain the process to the worker and use consistent treatment.

## Check the control itself

Test whether reminders reach the deputy during absence. Periodically compare your follow-up list with the underlying records; a complete-looking dashboard can still contain an incorrect date.
