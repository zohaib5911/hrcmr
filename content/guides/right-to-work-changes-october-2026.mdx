## What was verified

The Home Office employer guide dated 1 October 2026 confirms changes to the scope of the Right to Work Scheme. Review the current guide rather than relying on a pre-October checklist.

## Review working arrangements

The scheme now covers working arrangements beyond traditional employment. The direct contractual relationship remains important; extended liability can also apply in a contractual chain. Assess the actual arrangement and the commencement conditions, not just labels such as contractor or agency worker. Earlier contracts are not brought into extended liability solely because work continues after 1 October.

## Check digital providers

From 1 October, a prescribed digital check using a digital verification service requires the relevant certification and statutory-register listing. This does not make every online identity product a valid right to work service.

## Update your process

Map the parties in each arrangement, identify the applicable checking requirements, and document the assurance you obtain. An upstream organisation should not assume that a supplier warranty alone satisfies the prescribed requirements. Obtain specialist advice for a complex chain. Keep your recruitment team and procurement team working from the same reviewed procedure.
