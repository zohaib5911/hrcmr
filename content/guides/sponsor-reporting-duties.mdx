## Recognise an event promptly

Sponsor reporting covers specified changes affecting workers and the organisation. Train managers to flag changes to the sponsorship team immediately; an HR update does not itself submit a Home Office report.

## Use the applicable rule

Part 3 of the sponsor guidance generally requires listed worker changes within 10 working days, with exceptions and event-specific triggers. Organisational changes have their own rules; specified ownership changes can require action within 20 working days. Do not apply one deadline to every event. Check the relevant paragraph and route guidance, including whether permission or a new application is needed before a role changes.

## Make the report traceable

Record the event date, applicable rule, deadline, responsible user and evidence supporting the report. Retain the submission confirmation and reconcile it with the internal record. Give urgent cases a deputy when the usual user is away.

## Reconcile routinely

Compare recent joiners, leavers and changes against the reporting log. Investigate discrepancies promptly. If a deadline may have been missed, seek qualified advice and record the corrective action; changing an internal date does not repair a missed report.
