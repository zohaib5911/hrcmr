## Prepare evidence, not a presentation

A useful internal review asks whether another trained person can find the evidence and explain the process. Choose a small sample of worker records, trace each from recruitment to its current position, and list gaps with an owner and a target date.

## Build a review pack

Use Appendix D to identify the records relevant to your routes. Check that right to work evidence, contact details, absences and role records can be retrieved securely. Reconcile records across HR, payroll and the sponsorship management system rather than treating one system as automatically correct.

## Practise the handover

Ask the deputy to retrieve the sample without help from the usual administrator. Record where they struggle. Fix access and training gaps while keeping sensitive worker information within authorised access.

## Use the self-test

The 26 questions below are our internal preparation prompts, not an official Home Office questionnaire. Answer Yes only where you can show evidence; use Not sure when you have not tested the control. Treat every gap as an action even if your overall score is high. The score cannot predict an inspection outcome or certify compliance.
